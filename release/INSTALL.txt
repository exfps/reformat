Reformat 1.1.0 — macOS (Apple Silicon)

1. Open Reformat_1.1.0_aarch64.dmg.
2. Drag Reformat into Applications.
3. The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized. On first launch, Control-click
   Reformat in Applications and choose Open.

If macOS still blocks it after that, run:

  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Reformat.app

Requires macOS 11 or newer on Apple Silicon.

No account. No upload. Your files are processed on this Mac.
